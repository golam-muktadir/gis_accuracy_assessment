Rules of Thumb

Pixels vs. Clusters

Unit: Single pixel (sensitive to GPS error) vs. Pixel clusters.

Size: Rule-of-thumb minimum of ~50 sample points per map class (e.g., 5 classes = 250 min points).

Concept 03

Simple Random Sampling (SRS)

Points are scattered entirely at random across the entire study area.

Click to Flip ➔
Pros & Cons

The Rare Class Trap

Pros: Statistically unbiased and simple to implement.

Cons: Highly inefficient for rare classes (e.g., wetlands covering <2% area). Often receives zero points!

Concept 04

Stratified Random Sampling

The study area is divided into strata based on map classes before drawing random points.

Click to Flip ➔
The Gold Standard

Guaranteed Representation

Ensures rare or minority classes get adequate sample size representation. Requires post-stratification weighting when calculating overall map error matrices.

Concept 05

Systematic & Cluster Sampling

Regular grid layouts or grouped pixel blocks used to optimize field data collection.

Click to Flip ➔
Trade-offs

Coverage vs. Autocorrelation

Systematic: Great spatial coverage, but risks bias if landscape has periodic patterns.

Cluster: Reduces travel time in fieldwork, but spatial autocorrelation violates i.i.d. assumptions.
